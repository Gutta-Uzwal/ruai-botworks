Reporting structure

Who reports to whom, and who can stop a release

Three lines leave the CEO. One builds, one runs the company, and one assesses data protection independently of both.

Why merge-authority reports to testing-architect. It reviews the architects' work, so it must not report to them. That single edge is why verification cannot be overruled by the team it verifies — and it is why the reviewer holds no Write tool at all.
Why the Data Protection Officer reports to you directly. GDPR Art. 38(3) requires the DPO to reach the highest management level and to take no instruction on how to perform the role. Routing it through coo or cto would make the independence nominal. It raises findings; quality-compliance blocks on them; only you unblock.
