# School public site requirements

The first release provides public pages for the school identity, about content,
admissions, academics, campus life, events, and contact.

It must support responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, accessible labels and
focus states, metadata, sitemap and robots rules. The contact response must not
expose student or applicant records, and no public page may contain personally
identifiable information about minors.

Student, parent, teacher, and administrator authentication, grades, attendance,
private messaging, and enrollment records are explicitly deferred.
