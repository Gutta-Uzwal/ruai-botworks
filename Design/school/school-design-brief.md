# School design brief

Domain narrowing of [`../premium-web-design-standard.md`](../premium-web-design-standard.md).
Applies to every school-domain build, starting with
[`Templates/school/premium-v1`](../../Templates/school/premium-v1/).

## Emotional target

A parent should leave the homepage believing "I can trust this school with my
child," then "this school is academically serious," then want to click Apply
or Book a Visit. The site must not read as a corporate SaaS landing page, a
government portal, or a dated school-website template.

## Color direction

Not automatically blue. Pick one direction per school and commit in DESIGN.md:

- **Premium academic** — deep navy, warm ivory, muted gold, charcoal
- **Modern international** — deep green, cream, terracotta, warm neutral
- **Contemporary** — deep blue, soft sky, white, warm accent
- **Heritage** — burgundy, cream, forest, gold

## Imagery

Photography shows real-looking learning, not staged corporate stock: students
in class, teachers interacting, sports, arts, science, campus, friendships.
Calm and premium, not busy — motion on school/student imagery stays subtle.

## Homepage architecture

1. Hero — cinematic campus/student imagery, a headline that is not
   "Welcome to [School Name]," primary + secondary CTA (e.g. "Explore Our
   School" / "Book a Campus Visit")
2. Trust — years of excellence, students, faculty, accreditations, animated
   on scroll-into-view
3. School story — editorial, one strong statement + large imagery
4. Academics — interactive selection (hover/select a stage changes image +
   copy), not four identical cards
5. Educational philosophy — large typography-driven statement
6. Campus — immersive photography of labs, library, sports, arts, tech
7. Student life — clubs, sports, arts, leadership, community service
8. Faculty — a few featured educators, not a repetitive grid
9. Testimonials — parent/student voice, photo optional
10. News & events — editorial layout, one featured + secondary stories
11. Admissions — a 5-step visual journey (discover → visit → apply →
    assess → enroll), high-conversion
12. Final CTA — one strong closing section, then the footer

## Navigation

Logo · About · Academics · Campus · Student Life · Admissions · News, with an
"Apply Now" CTA that stays visible. Mobile: drawer menu + persistent Apply CTA.
